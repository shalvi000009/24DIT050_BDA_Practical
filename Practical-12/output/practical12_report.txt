1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM DEFINITION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Modern retail operations process thousands of daily transactions across diverse geographical 
regions, product categories, and customer segments. Static, non-interactive tabular reports 
fail to provide executive leadership with the real-time exploratory capability required to 
identify revenue drivers, optimize inventory, detect regional underperformance, and flag 
loss-leader product lines.

This practical constructs an interactive Customer Sales Analytics Dashboard using Python, 
Streamlit, and Plotly. The dashboard ingests a multi-year Superstore Sales dataset, executes 
data quality validation, engineers temporal and financial metrics, and presents an 
executive dashboard equipped with real-time multi-dimensional cross-filtering.

3. MULTI-DIMENSIONAL KPI DEFINITIONS & FORMULAS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The executive dashboard monitors 7 primary Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):

1. Total Sales ($)           : Sum of net revenue across all filtered orders.
                               Formula: SUM(Sales) = $13,641,279.68

2. Total Profit ($)          : Sum of net profit across all filtered orders.
                               Formula: SUM(Profit) = $2,122,428.16

3. Distinct Orders (#)       : Count of unique order IDs (excluding row duplication).
                               Formula: COUNT_DISTINCT(Order_ID) = 4,997

4. Distinct Customers (#)    : Count of unique customer accounts.
                               Formula: COUNT_DISTINCT(Customer_ID) = 20

5. Total Units Sold (#)      : Total quantity of physical items purchased.
                               Formula: SUM(Quantity) = 27,817 units

6. Average Order Value ($)   : Average revenue generated per distinct order.
                               Formula: Total_Sales / Distinct_Orders = $2,729.89

7. Profit Margin (%)         : Percentage of total sales retained as net profit.
                               Formula: (Total_Profit / Total_Sales) * 100 = 15.56%
